- The template's design guards are installed verbatim from tag v0.08.002 (ADR-0078): `scripts/check_sdd.py` replaces the
  older guard (executive brief, research basis, sizing and contracts, technology and versions, resources, one
  `validation.md` per feature, a gate that is a live test or a script) and `scripts/check_tech_matrix.py` is new (the
  technology table is the pinned dependencies). CI runs both in the guards job, which now installs the web packages and
  `jsonschema` first because the SDD guard validates the executive view against the shell's installed schema. Both fail on
  the current design document until it is written to them; their findings are recorded for that work.
- The planted-defect suite, `tests/adversarial/`: every guard CI runs rejects a defect planted in a temporary copy of the
  repository and passes on the clean copy, and no plant writes into the real tree. It is the template's suite adapted
  to Fragmenta (a synthetic tree for the SDD, technology, version and budget guards; plants on the real catalog for the
  contract, readiness and skeleton guards; plants for `check_guards`, `check_doc_paths`, `check_content_standards`,
  `check_spanish_accents`, `check_deploy_place`, `check_version_coherence`, `check_documents`, `check_web_baseline` and the
  merged `check_artifacts`). CI runs it as `pip install pytest==9.1.1 && python -m pytest tests/adversarial -q`, the one
  suite `check_ci_budget` allows. The two real-tree tests of the design guards carry a strict `xfail` until the design
  document passes them.
- The architecture modal describes the 0.08.000 product in both languages: the Introduction as the landing with its
  executive view, the App at `/app`, Focus mode inside the App (`?focus=1`) instead of a separate focus page, twelve
  Implementation topics in four groups, and one sentence on the contract catalog and the readiness document. The drawings
  are regenerated by `scripts/build_architecture_svgs.py`, and two comments that described the retired focus page
  (`frontend/src/fragmenta.css`, `frontend/vite.config.ts`) are corrected.
- `docs/design/measured-resources.md` records the product's measured resources with the command and output of each: the
  stored size of `data/derived`, the bake's wall time and peak memory from a bake into a throwaway root, the tests, the
