# Guides

Task-oriented pages. Each says what to run, what it writes or shows, and what to check.

1. [Running the bake](guides/01_running-the-bake.md): baking the cases, the models and the benchmark;
   the release gate; determinism; upgrading the engine.
2. [Bringing your own blasts](guides/02_your-own-blasts.md): the engine on blasts that are not in the
   corpus, what each arm needs, and what to expect from the corpus models at a new mine.
3. [Reading a number](guides/03_reading-a-number.md): which statistic, which protocol, which row set,
   what was withheld, and what an interval resamples.
4. [Designing a blast](guides/04_what-if.md): the response surface over burden and spacing, and every model on a changed design.
5. [Regenerating the docs and the drawings](guides/05_regenerating.md): the results pages, the fact
   blocks, the architecture drawings and the exported figures.
