# Requirements: the synthetic cases are not circular

Designed 2026-10-05, before its code (ADR-0075), for backlog item BL-027. The research plan had the synthetic cases
carry a truth generated by a model different from every arm; as built, they carry no measured size at all and are
designs, so no arm can be scored on its own output. The one deliberate exception is the positive control, which is
circular by design and says so. EARS.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| NC-001 | THE synthetic design cases SHALL carry no measured size, so no arm is scored on them. | `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_no_synthetic_design_carries_a_measured_size_or_a_score` |
| NC-002 | THE benchmark SHALL be computed on the corpus blasts only, and no synthetic blast SHALL enter it. | `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_no_synthetic_blast_enters_the_benchmark` |
| NC-003 | THE synthetic designs SHALL not depend on any arm: building them SHALL succeed with every arm's prediction disabled. | `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_the_synthetic_designs_do_not_depend_on_any_arm` |
| NC-004 | THE positive control's truth SHALL be the published regression's own output, and ITS case SHALL say that it tests the harness, not the science. | `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_the_positive_control_is_circular_by_design_and_says_so` |
