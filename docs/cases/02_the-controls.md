# The controls

Four cases exist so the product refuses, stamps or recovers instead of answering freely. Each control is
attached only to the case that can trigger it, and each one checks a specific way a plausible number
could reach a screen without anything behind it.

| Control | Case | What must happen | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| geometry negative | `real-miami` | every arm that needs a rock volume abstains | 0 of the geometry-needing arms answered on the 6 blasts |
| degenerate negative | `ctrl-degenerate` | every arm refuses a design with no charge column | all 13 arms refused all 6 designs (78 of 78 cells) |
| extrapolation | `real-granite-ne` | every prediction outside the envelope is stamped | 40 predictions, none unstamped |
| positive | `ctrl-oracle` | a model recovers the truth it generated | 8 blasts checked, largest error 0.0 m |

The counts are those of the committed artifacts; the release gate fails the bake if any control fails.

---

## 1. The geometry negative control: Miami

Six blasts whose absolute scale no source publishes: no hole diameter and no dimension of any kind, so
nothing fixes the scale. Every arm that needs a rock volume and a charge mass abstains there, with a
reason naming what is missing, and a test asserts that none answered. The ratio-only arms (the
published regression, the learned arms) still answer.

This campaign is also what separates the benchmark's two row sets: the classical arms cannot be scored
on it, so every grouped score is reported with and without it
([protocols/04](../protocols/04_criterion-and-row-sets.md)).

## 2. The degenerate negative control

Six designs where the stemming exceeds the bench, so there is no charge column and the design is not a
blast. Every arm refuses, including the ratio-only ones: a power law over seven ratios has no term that
knows the stemming has taken the whole hole, and it would return a number. So the guard sits at the
design level, before any model runs, and a test asserts that every arm refused every design. The
uniformity index on these designs is exactly zero, because it is linear in the charge length.

## 3. The extrapolation control: the granite field set

Five production blasts at a granite mine in north-east China (Sui et al. 2025), with a Young modulus of
5.6 GPa against a corpus minimum of 9.57, on the feature both 2025 studies rank first. Every prediction
on them must carry the extrapolation stamp, and the control counts the stamps on the predictions
themselves rather than a flag on the case, because the stamp reaching the reader is what matters.

The set also publishes its absolute pattern with its ratios, so it is the one real set where the
geometry reconstruction is checked against a published answer ([data/03](../data/03_geometry.md)).

## 4. The positive control

Truth generated by a known model, which that same model must recover. It tests the apparatus, not the
science: if the model that produced these numbers cannot reproduce them, the plumbing is wrong and no
other number on the site can be trusted. The measured recovery error is zero.

---

## What the controls do not show

None of them says a model is right. The geometry control does not show the reconstruction is correct;
the fifteen dimensional constraints do ([data/03](../data/03_geometry.md)). The degenerate control does
not show the models are calibrated; the benchmark measures that, and its answer is mostly negative. The
positive control says nothing about blasting. Each closes one route by which a number with nothing behind
it could be shown, and they run on every bake.
