# The controls

Four cases exist so that the product **refuses** rather than answers, and they are the first ones to
look at when judging whether it is honest.

Each control is attached only to the cases that can trigger it. A control block on every case would
look thorough and mean nothing.

---

## The geometry negative control

**Six blasts whose absolute scale no source publishes.** One of the ten campaigns gives no hole
diameter and no absolute dimension of any kind, so nothing fixes its scale.

Every model that needs a rock volume and a charge mass **abstains** there, with a reason string
naming the campaign and saying what is missing. A test asserts that none of them answered.

A number there would be an invention. This is the case that keeps the classical models honest, and it
is why the geometry module raises rather than returning a plausible default.

## The degenerate negative control

**Six designs where the stemming exceeds the bench**, so there is no charge column and the design is
not a blast at all.

Every model refuses, **including the ratio-only ones**. That is the part worth understanding: nothing
in a power law over seven dimensionless ratios knows that the stemming has swallowed the hole. It
will happily price it and return a confident number.

So the guard sits at the **design level**, before any model runs, rather than at the number level
after one has. A test asserts that all twelve models refused all six designs.

## The extrapolation control

**Five field blasts below the corpus minimum on its most important feature.** Their Young modulus is
5.6 GPa against a corpus minimum of 9.57, and both 2025 studies rank that feature first.

Every prediction on them is **stamped** as an extrapolation and badged on screen. The control asserts
that all 35 predictions carry the stamp, which is a stronger check than asserting the case is
flagged: it is the badge reaching the reader that matters, not the flag existing in a manifest.

This set is also the only real one that publishes its **absolute** pattern as well as its ratios, so
it doubles as the independent check on the geometry reconstruction rather than a consumer of it.

## The positive control

**Truth generated by a known model, which that same model must then recover.** It tests the
apparatus rather than the science: if the model that produced these numbers cannot reproduce them,
the plumbing is broken and no other result on the site can be trusted.

Measured recovery error: zero, to within floating-point exactness.

---

## What a control cannot do

It cannot tell you a model is right. It can only tell you that a specific way of being wrong is not
happening.

The geometry control does not say the reconstruction is correct; the fifteen narrative constraints do
that. The degenerate control does not say the models are calibrated; the benchmark does that, and its
answer is largely negative. The positive control says nothing at all about blasting.

They are cheap, they run on every bake, and each one closes a route by which a plausible number could
reach a screen with nothing behind it.
